## Package 3 — Annual Compliance Package

**Trigger:** Start of each financial year (typically April) or as mandated by Income Tax Dept.  
**Deadline:** Before 31 January of the financial year for Form 12BB (to compute advance TDS)  
**Responsible:** HR / Accounts collects; employee fills

| Document | File | Governing Law | Submitted To | Key Fields |
|----------|------|---------------|--------------|------------|
| Consent Letter for Tax Regime | Consent and Form-12BB_F.Y_2023-2024_.xlsx | IT Act S.115BAC | HR / Accounts | Name, PAN, employee code, branch, regime choice (Old/New), signature |
| Form 12BB (Investment Declaration) | Consent and Form-12BB_F.Y_2023-2024_.xlsx | Rule 26C of IT Rules | HR / Accounts | HRA (rent + landlord PAN), LTA, Home loan interest (lender PAN), 80C–80CCD, 80E/G/TTA |

> **Note:** If no consent is submitted, the new tax regime is applied by default from FY 2023-24 onwards per S.115BAC.  
> Form 12BB is required ONLY for employees opting the Old Tax Regime.

## Package 4 — Probation Review Package

**Trigger:** End of probation period (typically 6 months–1 year from DOJ)  
**Responsible:** Reporting Authority → Review Authorities → HR → MD  
**Purpose:** Structured multi-level review to confirm, extend, or terminate probation

### 4A — Appraisal Forms (by role track)

| Document | File | Role Track | Criteria |
|----------|------|------------|----------|
| Confirmation Appraisal Form (Technical) | Sudesna Singha Roy.docx | Chemist / Technical | Punctuality, Discipline, Learning Ability, Analytical Skills, Integrity, Work Efficiency, Communication, Accuracy, Pressure handling, Developmental Activities + Chemistry test |
| Confirmation Appraisal Form (Operations) | Uma Paul.docx | Operations / Field | Punctuality, Discipline/Behavior, Motivation, Integrity, Communication, Knowledge of Procedures, Reporting, Initiative, Time Management, Mobility + Chemistry score |

**Rating Scale:** 5 = Excellent → 0 = Failed. Total: 50 marks.  
**Critical Rule:** Integrity score must be 5 (Excellent) for confirmation to be approved at any level.

**Approval Chain:**
```
Reporting Authority
    → Review Authority 1
        → Review Authority 2
            → Review Authority 3
                → HR (hike recommendation)
                    → Managing Director (final approval)
```

## Recommended Onboarding Form Sequence

```
Week 0 (Offer)
  └── HR issues Offer Letter
       └── Candidate signs and returns; brings joining documents on Day 1

Day 1
  ├── [Internal]
  │     ├── Application Form Permanent (5 pages) — employee fills
  │     ├── Bank Details Form — employee fills + attaches passbook/cheque
  │     ├── Appointment Letter (signed copy) — employee signs and returns
  │     └── Declaration of Confidentiality and Impartiality — employee signs
  │
  └── [Statutory — must be submitted within 10 days]
        ├── Form A — PF Membership Declaration (internal PF trust)
        ├── Form B — PF Nomination (internal PF trust)
        ├── Form 1 — ESIC Declaration (to ESIC Branch Office)
        ├── Form 11 — Composite EPF/EPS Declaration (to EPFO via employer)
        └── Gratuity Form F — Nomination (retained by employer)

Annual (each FY, before Jan 31)
  └── Form 12BB + Consent Letter (Old/New Tax Regime) — to HR/Accounts

End of Probation (6–12 months)
  ├── HR initiates Confirmation Appraisal Form (role-specific)
  ├── Multi-level approval chain completed
  └── HR issues:
        ├── Confirmation Letter (+ revised CTC Annexure if hike)
        OR
        └── Extension of Probation Letter (with review date)
```
